Our Procedures

Root Canal Treatment

One of the most common dental procedures performed, with well over 14 million every year. This simple treatment can save your natural teeth and prevent the need for dental implants or bridges.

Root Canal Therapy

Endodontic therapy can locate fractures or hidden canals that do not appear on x-rays. Damaged root surfaces or surrounding bone may also be treated, including apicoectomy (root-end resection).

Endodontic Retreatment

If a previous root canal is causing discomfort, retreatment can address healing issues and restore your tooth's health using advanced microscope-assisted techniques.

Cracked Teeth

Precise diagnosis and treatment of cracked teeth to relieve pain and prevent further damage, using the latest digital imaging and microscopy technology.

Traumatic Injuries

Prompt, expert care for teeth affected by accidents or trauma — we work to preserve your natural tooth whenever possible.
